CLASS ACTION

A game about what it is like to be the working class or the capitalist

At the start of the game you are given a class at random. If you get the bourgeoisie you own businesses and apartments, you set pay and working conditions, and you collect rent and profit. If you get the proletariat you have to take a job, rent a place to live, and pay your bills from your wages. Press play and see which one you get.
